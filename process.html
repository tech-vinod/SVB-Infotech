Our Training to Placement Process

A battle-tested corporate framework that systematically transforms candidates into industry-ready software engineers through rigorous hands-on practice.

01

Skill Profiling & Assessment

1-on-1 counseling with technical leads to identify your aptitudes, career ambitions, and select the optimal technology pathway (Full Stack, Python AI, Cloud, or Java).

Outcome: Personalized Learning Roadmap
02

100% Practical Deep Dive

Zero boring theoretical lectures. Daily hands-on coding drills, live debugging on modern IDEs, and architecture walkthroughs by senior corporate practitioners.

Outcome: 50+ Real Coding Exercises
03

Live Client Capstone Projects

Work on real client deliverables with modular codebases, microservice APIs, database schemas, CI/CD automation, and cloud deployments on AWS/Vercel.

Outcome: 2+ Production Deployed Projects
04

Resume & GitHub Portfolio

Our career coaches overhaul your resume to pass ATS filters, polish your GitHub repositories with comprehensive READMEs, and optimize your LinkedIn presence.

Outcome: High-Impact Tech Resume & GitHub
05

Mock Technical & HR Rounds

Rigorous simulation of tier-1 IT recruitment rounds. Live algorithmic problem solving, machine coding rounds, system design discussions, and HR behavioral rehearsals.

Outcome: Zero Interview Fear
